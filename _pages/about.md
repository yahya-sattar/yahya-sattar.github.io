Foundational AI Postdoctoral Fellow, <a href='https://www.cornell.edu/about/'>Cornell University</a> (<a href='https://www.cs.cornell.edu'>Department of Computer Science</a>).  

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow with the Department of Computer Science at Cornell University, working with <a href='https://sdean.website'>Prof. Sarah Dean</a>. I received my PhD in Electrical Engineering under the supervision of <a href='https://sota.engin.umich.edu'>Prof. Samet Oymak</a> at University of California Riverside, where my research focused on <a href='https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Yahya-Sattar-3/publication/376076888_thesis/links/6568cf00ce88b870312083ab/thesis.pdf'>statistical learning and adaptive control of complex dynamical systems</a>. 


My research studies the fundamental limits of <strong><font color="FireBrick">learning-based control</font></strong>, <strong><font color="FireBrick">autoregressive prediction</font></strong>, and <strong><font color="FireBrick">world modeling</font></strong> in  <strong><font color="CornflowerBlue">nonlinear</font></strong>, <strong><font color="CornflowerBlue">uncertain</font></strong>, and <strong><font color="CornflowerBlue">partially observed</font></strong> settings, with applications in robotics, autonomous systems, and broader scientific and engineering domains. More precisely, I develop <strong><font color="FireBrick">theoretical foundations</font></strong> and <strong><font color="FireBrick">efficient algorithms</font></strong> for learning nonlinear dynamics and latent representations from sequential data, and for using the learned dynamics and representations to predict, plan, and control. My work connects <strong><font color="FireBrick">nonlinear system identification</font></strong>, <strong><font color="FireBrick">adaptive control</font></strong>, <strong><font color="FireBrick">reinforcement learning</font></strong>, <strong><font color="FireBrick">autoregressive prediction</font></strong> and <strong><font color="FireBrick">world modeling</font></strong>, drawing on tools from <strong><font color="CornflowerBlue">statistical learning</font></strong>, <strong><font color="CornflowerBlue">optimization</font></strong>, and <strong><font color="CornflowerBlue">control theory</font></strong>.
