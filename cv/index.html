Summary My research studies the fundamental limits of learning-based control, autoregressive prediction, and world modeling in nonlinear, uncertain, and partially observed settings. I develop theoretical foundations and efficient algorithms for learning nonlinear dynamics and latent representations from sequential data, and for using the learned dynamics and representations to predict, plan, and control. My work connects nonlinear system identification, adaptive control, reinforcement learning, autoregressive prediction and world modeling, drawing on tools from statistical learning, optimization, and control theory.
